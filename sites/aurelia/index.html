The Aurelia way

Three pathways to restoration

Every visit is built around sensory care — touch, water, and botanical nourishment working in concert.

Therapeutic Massage

Warm-stone, deep-tissue, and lymphatic drainage performed in low light with weighted linens and slow, deliberate rhythm.

Hydrotherapy Circuit

Mineral pools, cedar saunas, and cold plunges sequenced to open circulation and prepare the body for deeper rest.

Botanical Facials

Cold-pressed plant oils and clay masks tailored to your skin, applied with stone rollers and unhurried hands.

Water, warmth, breath

The hydrotherapy circuit

Begin in the warm mineral pool, move through the cedar sauna, and finish with a guided cold plunge. The sequence draws blood to the surface, releases held tension, and slows the nervous system into a parasympathetic state — the foundation for everything that follows.

  • Three thermal stages across 45 minutes
  • Mineral-rich water sourced from a natural spring
  • Guided breathwork between each stage
  • Pairs seamlessly with any massage or facial
