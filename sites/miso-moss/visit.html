Our Philosophy

A room built for slow evenings and close conversation

Miso & Moss exists for the moments that deserve more than a meal. Every surface — the charcoal walls, the warm glow of binchotan embers, the gold-rimmed glassware — is designed to pull you out of the city and into something quieter. We built an izakaya that doesn't shout. It whispers.

Our small plates arrive in deliberate sequence. Our sake list runs deep and rare. And the room itself, lit by nothing but candle and grill-fire, asks you to stay a little longer than you planned.

  • Bincho-grilled skewers fired over white charcoal for clean, smoky depth
  • A rotating sake cellar of 40+ labels, many unavailable anywhere else in the city
  • An intimate 32-seat room designed around candlelight and shadow
  • A tasting menu crafted nightly from what's seasonal, local, and exceptional

Find Us
