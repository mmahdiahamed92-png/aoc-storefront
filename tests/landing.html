FAQ

Questions, answered

How fast are transfers, really?

Most payments settle in under 30 seconds. A small number of corridors may take up to 60 minutes during local banking holidays, but you'll always see an estimated arrival time before confirming.

What does it actually cost?

You pay a flat fee of 0.8% per transaction, capped at $5. There are no subscription fees, no monthly minimums, and no charge for holding balances in supported currencies.

Do I need a business bank account?

No. NovaPay works for individuals and registered entities alike. You can receive payments to a personal account and withdraw to any linked bank or wallet in your name.

Is my money safe while it's in NovaPay?

Funds are held in segregated accounts at regulated partner banks and never used for lending. NovaPay is registered with financial authorities in every market we operate in.
