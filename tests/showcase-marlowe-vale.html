Our Approach

Built on Patience, Defined by Taste

Marlowe & Vale was founded on a simple conviction: that the most extraordinary homes deserve representation with equal parts rigour and restraint. We are not a volume brokerage. We take on a limited number of properties each year, accepting only those we would be proud to associate our name with — homes where the architecture is honest, the setting is exceptional, and the experience of being inside is quietly transformative.

We don't sell houses. We represent the spaces people have spent a lifetime working toward.

Our team brings together former architects, art advisors, and finance professionals who understand that a great property is both a residence and an asset. We advise on acquisition, disposition, and long-term portfolio strategy with the same standard of care our clients apply to every other dimension of their lives. The result is a practice built almost entirely on referral, reputation, and the quiet satisfaction of doing the work properly.

FAQ

Frequently Asked

Do you only represent waterfront and architectural properties?
We specialise in waterfront estates, penthouse residences, and architecturally significant homes. This focus allows us to maintain deep expertise and a highly targeted buyer network. If your property doesn't fall within these categories, we're happy to recommend a trusted colleague who may be a better fit.
How do you access properties that aren't publicly listed?
Over twenty years we've built relationships with private sellers, developers, architects, and family offices. Many of the most desirable homes change hands without ever reaching the open market. Our buyers gain access to these properties through direct, discreet introductions.
What does your listing presentation include?
Every Signature Listing receives an editorial-grade package: professional architectural photography, a cinematic property film, a monograph-quality property book, and a digital presentation designed in-house. We treat each home as a story worth telling beautifully.
How long does a typical sale take?
Our average closing period is 48 days from listing to accepted offer, though architecturally significant or higher-value properties may take longer to find the right buyer. We prioritise the right outcome over speed, and our 98% list-to-sale price ratio reflects that discipline.

Whether you are searching for a home that has not yet been found or considering the sale of a property you have spent years perfecting, we would welcome a private discussion. No obligation, no pressure — simply a beginning.
