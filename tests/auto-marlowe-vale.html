Our Philosophy

A house is a building. A home is a point of view.

Marlowe & Vale was founded on a simple conviction: that the most extraordinary residences are never just square footage and zip codes — they are statements of intent. We built our practice around a small number of clients and an even smaller number of properties, because attention is the one luxury that cannot be manufactured. Every listing we represent is chosen for its architectural integrity, its relationship to the landscape, and the way it makes a person feel the moment they walk through the door.

Our team brings together backgrounds in architecture, fine art, and private wealth advisory — not just real estate. That means we can speak fluently about the cantilever system of a hillside home, the provenance of a mid-century renovation, or the tax implications of a cross-border purchase. We exist for clients who expect the person selling their home to understand it as deeply as the architect who drew it.

FAQ

Questions, considered

Do you work with buyers who are not yet ready to purchase?
Absolutely. Many of our best acquisitions begin as year-long conversations. We maintain relationships with clients long before a transaction is on the table — it's how we ensure the right property is found, not just the available one.
How do you handle properties that should never be publicly listed?
A significant portion of our portfolio is handled discreetly, off-market. We maintain a private register of vetted buyers and sellers who require confidentiality, and we facilitate introductions only when both parties are ready.
What geographic areas do you cover?
Our primary focus is waterfront and architecturally significant properties along the coast and in major metropolitan centres. We also work with an international network of partner agencies for clients with cross-border requirements.
How is your commission structured?
Our commission reflects the scope of marketing, the uniqueness of the property, and the complexity of the transaction. We discuss this transparently during the initial consultation, before any commitment is made.
