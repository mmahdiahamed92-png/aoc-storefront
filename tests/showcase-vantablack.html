The label

Started in a bedroom. Cut in a warehouse. Worn worldwide.

VANTABLACK began in 2019 when our founder, frustrated with streetwear that looked incredible on Instagram and fell apart in three months, started cutting and sewing tees on a single secondhand machine in a South London bedsit. The name came from the coating that absorbs 99.96% of light — the idea was to make garments so black and so dense they felt like a void you could wear. No logos screaming for attention. No seasonal colour stories. Just weight, structure and a matte finish that held up.

We don't make clothes for everyone. We make them for the people who notice the difference between 300 and 380gsm.

Five years later we operate out of a warehouse in Hackney Wick, run our own small-batch dye program, and ship numbered drops to collectors in 30+ countries. We've never taken outside investment, never wholesaled, and never reprinted a sold-out drop. If you missed it, you missed it — that scarcity is the point, not a marketing trick. Every garment carries a drop number and a production count stitched inside the hem.

FAQ

Questions we get every drop cycle.

Do you ever reprint sold-out pieces?
Never. Each drop is numbered and produced in a fixed quantity. Once it's gone, it's archived. We occasionally release evolved versions of past silhouettes with different construction or fabric, but the original piece is not reissued.
Why is everything black?
It's the entire thesis of the label. We explore the full range of what black can be — matte, glossy, textured, tonal, raised — across different weights and materials. Colour would be a distraction from the construction details we want you to notice.
How does sizing run across your cuts?
Our tees are cut boxy with a dropped shoulder — if you're between sizes and prefer a fitted look, size down. Outerwear runs true to size and is designed to layer over our heavyweight tees. Full measurements are on every product page.
What's the difference between Standard Access and Black List?
Standard Access is free and gives you 48-hour notice before a drop. Black List costs £18/month and gives you a 24-hour early window, a guaranteed piece in your size, member-only colourways, and free UK shipping. If you've missed drops before, Black List exists to make sure you don't.
