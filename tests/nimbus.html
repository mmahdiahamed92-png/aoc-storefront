FAQ

Questions, answered

How does Nimbus handle different time zones and availability?

Nimbus detects each team member's working hours and current workload, then schedules and assigns tasks to respect availability — so no one gets pinged at 2 AM or buried under unrealistic deadlines.

Can I override the AI's plans and assignments?

Absolutely. Nimbus is your co-pilot, not your boss. Every plan, assignment, and deadline is fully editable. You can accept AI suggestions wholesale or fine-tune anything with a click.

What integrations does Nimbus support?

Nimbus connects natively with Slack, GitHub, Figma, Notion, Google Calendar, Linear, and Jira. A robust REST API is also available for custom workflows on Enterprise plans.

Is my company data secure?

Yes. Nimbus is SOC 2 Type II certified and uses end-to-end encryption for all data in transit and at rest. We never train AI models on your proprietary project data.
