Our story

No grey area

VANTABLACK started in a south London garage with one rule: make clothing with the intensity of the city that built it. We don't do pastels. We don't do safe. Every garment is cut, sampled and stress-tested before it earns the label. The result is a uniform for people who treat the street like a runway and the runway like a battlefield.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you restock sold-out items?

Never. Every drop is a limited run of 300 units or fewer. Once it sells out, it's archived for good. This keeps every piece rare and every owner early.

What's your shipping turnaround?

All orders ship within 48 hours of purchase via tracked courier. UK delivery takes 2–3 working days, international 5–10. You'll get a tracking link the moment it leaves the warehouse.

How do I find my size?

Our tees run boxy and oversized by design. If you want a fitted look, size down. Check the size chart on each product page for exact garment measurements — not body measurements.

What's your return policy?

Unworn items with tags intact can be returned within 14 days for a full refund. Final-sale items from clearance drops are non-returnable. We'll exchange for a different size if stock allows.
