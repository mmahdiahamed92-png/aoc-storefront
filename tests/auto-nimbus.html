FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does Nimbus know who should get which task?
Nimbus builds a profile for each teammate based on their skills, past task history, current workload, and time zone. When a task is generated, it's routed to the best match automatically — and you can always override or adjust the rules.
Does Nimbus work with the tools we already use?
Yes. Nimbus integrates natively with GitHub, Linear, Jira, Figma, Slack, Google Workspace, and Notion. It pulls progress signals from all of them so your dashboard stays live without manual updates.
Is our data used to train AI models?
Never. Your project data, task content, and team communications are encrypted and isolated. We do not use customer data to train shared models. Scale plan customers can also deploy custom models trained exclusively on their environment.
How long does it take to get set up?
Most teams are fully onboarded in under 20 minutes. Connect your integrations, invite your team, describe your first goal in plain language, and Nimbus generates a complete plan immediately.
