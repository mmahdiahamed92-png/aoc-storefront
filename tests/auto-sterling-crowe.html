Mergers & Acquisitions

From founder exits to cross-border roll-ups, we manage the full lifecycle of a transaction with the discretion and rigour the moment demands. We structure, negotiate, and close — protecting value at every turn.

  • Buy-side & sell-side advisory — We represent acquirers, sellers, and special committees through every stage — LOI, due diligence, negotiation, signing, and closing.
  • Founder exits & secondary sales — Tailored counsel for founders navigating secondary liquidity, earn-outs, and post-closing obligations that preserve both wealth and reputation.
  • Cross-border transactions — Coordinated multi-jurisdictional deal execution with a network of trusted international counsel across Europe, Asia, and North America.
02

Venture Financing

We have sat across the table from nearly every major fund in the ecosystem. That means we know what a clean term sheet looks like — and we know how to push back without killing the deal.

  • Priced equity rounds — Series A through pre-IPO, including down rounds, pay-to-play provisions, and complex recapitalisations handled with clarity and speed.
  • Convertible & SAFE instruments — Structuring and negotiating convertible notes, SAFEs, and bridge financings that protect founder economics while keeping the round moving.
  • Fund formation & LP side — Advising emerging fund managers on GP/LP structures, carried interest, side letters, and fund governance from first close through deployment.

Commercial Disputes

When stakes are high and the relationship is on the line, we bring a litigator's instinct and a dealmaker's pragmatism. We resolve complex disputes efficiently — through negotiation, arbitration, or, when necessary, the courtroom.

  • Shareholder & partnership disputes — Deadlock, oppression remedies, valuation disputes, and buyout negotiations handled with discretion and commercial sense.
  • Breach of contract & warranty claims — Strategic enforcement and defence of complex commercial contracts, including post-M&A warranty and indemnity claims.
  • Arbitration & mediation — International and domestic arbitration before major institutions, with a track record of resolving matters before they reach public dockets.

Sterling & Crowe was founded on a simple conviction: that the lawyers guiding a company through its most consequential moments should have done it hundreds of times before. Our partners each spent over a decade at top-tier international firms before choosing to build something more intimate — a practice where every client works directly with a partner, not a rotating cast of associates. We are not the firm for every legal need. We are the firm for the moments that determine whether a company becomes an institution — and we treat each one with the gravity it deserves.

FAQ

Questions We Hear Often

Do you work with companies at the pre-revenue stage?
Yes. A significant portion of our venture practice serves pre-revenue companies preparing for or actively raising their first institutional round. Our Founders' Counsel engagement is designed specifically for this stage.
How do you price transaction work?
For most defined transactions — an equity round, an acquisition, a major commercial agreement — we offer fixed-fee or capped-fee structures scoped in advance. You will know the total legal cost before we begin, with a clear breakdown of what is and isn't included.
Can you act for both sides in a negotiation?
No. We believe that uncompromised advocacy is non-negotiable. In any transaction we will represent one side exclusively and, where appropriate, refer the other party to trusted independent counsel.
What jurisdictions do you cover?
We are qualified and practise in England & Wales. For cross-border matters we coordinate a network of vetted international counsel across the EU, US, and APAC regions, maintaining lead counsel role and ensuring consistency throughout.
