FAQ

Questions, answered

What types of properties does Marlowe & Vale represent?

We focus exclusively on waterfront estates, penthouses and architecturally significant homes — typically priced from two million upward. If your property doesn't fit that profile, we're happy to recommend a trusted colleague.

Do you handle off-market and private sales?

Yes. A significant portion of our business is conducted privately. If you're selling and prefer not to list publicly, or buying and want access to unlisted opportunities, we can discuss how that works in a confidential conversation.

How many listings do you take on at once?

We cap our active portfolio at twelve properties. This ensures every home receives the full weight of our marketing, network and attention — never a diluted effort.

What areas do you cover?

Our primary markets are waterfront and architectural properties across Canada, with a particular focus on Ontario, British Columbia and Quebec. For international acquisitions we maintain a network of partner agencies in key markets.
