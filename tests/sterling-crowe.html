FAQ

Questions, answered

What stage companies do you typically work with?

We focus on growth-stage companies—generally Series A through pre-IPO—as well as the investors and boards that back them. If you're navigating a material transaction or dispute, we're likely a fit.

Do you handle cross-border transactions?

Yes. We regularly advise on multi-jurisdictional deals, coordinating with trusted local counsel where needed. Our partners have experience across the UK, EU, and US markets.

How do you approach fees?

We're transparent and flexible. Most transaction work is scoped on a fixed-fee or milestone basis; litigation and advisory matters may be hourly or hybrid. We discuss structure upfront—no surprises.

Will I work directly with partners?

Always. Every engagement is partner-led. You won't be passed to associates or filtered through layers. The person who scopes your matter is the person who handles it.
