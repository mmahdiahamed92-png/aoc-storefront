Our story

A Sanctuary Built Around Stillness

Aurelia was born from a simple belief: that rest is not a reward — it's a necessity. Tucked away from the noise, our retreat blends warm stone textures, natural light and living greenery into a space that asks nothing of you. Here, the pace slows. The breath deepens. And every treatment is an invitation to come home to yourself.

  • Locally sourced botanicals and organic skincare lines
  • Private treatment suites with natural light and sound buffering
  • Therapists trained in restorative and trauma-informed bodywork
  • Zero-screen zones throughout the entire retreat

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to book in advance?

We highly recommend booking ahead, especially for weekend and evening slots. Same-day availability is sometimes possible — call us and we'll do our best to welcome you in.

What should I bring?

Just yourself. We provide robes, sandals, towels and all treatment essentials. Arrive 15 minutes early to settle in and enjoy a cup of botanical tea before your session.

Can I book a full retreat day?

Absolutely. Our Half-Day and Full-Day Retreat packages combine multiple treatments with hydrotherapy access and a nourishing lunch. Perfect for solo restoration or a quiet day with someone close.

Is Aurelia suitable for first-time spa guests?

Completely. Our team will gently guide you through every step, and our zero-pressure environment means you set the tone. There's no expectation — only care.
